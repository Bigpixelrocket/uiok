## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- Scale tokens in `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/styles.css`, the same in every theme: type sizes (`--font-size-key` to `--font-size-heading`, and `--font-size-display` for a startup or splash title), line heights, weights, `--tracking-caps`, spacing (`--space-1` to `--space-16`), corners (`--radius-xs` to `--radius-pill`), line widths (`--line`, `--line-strong`), motion (`--duration-*`, `--ease-out`, `--ease-in-out`, `--transition-control`; the fast, base, and slow durations drop to zero under reduced motion), control, row, and bar heights (`--control-height`, `-sm`, `-xs`, `--row-height`, `--bar-height`, `--hit-target`), focus geometry (`--focus-ring-width`, `--focus-offset*`), icon sizes (`--icon-sm` to `--icon-xl`, `--icon-column`, `--dot`), and a fuller layer scale (`--z-below`, `--z-raised`, `--z-local-overlay`, `--z-menu`, `--z-drag`, `--z-overlay`, `--z-dialog-popover` beside the existing `--z-panel`, `--z-dialog`, `--z-tooltip`). See `docs/tokens.md`.
- Colour roles in every theme, each with a contrast floor: text roles (`--accent-text`, `--danger-text`, `--warning-text`, `--success-text`, `--text-dim-on-surface`, `--text-faint`, `--text-placeholder`), control outlines (`--control-border`, `--control-border-strong`, `--control-track`), washes and lines per hue (`--accent-wash`, `--danger-wash`, `--warning-line`, and their strong forms), pressed fills (`--primary-pressed`, `--tonal-pressed`, `--accent-pressed`, `--danger-pressed`, `--warning-pressed`, `--accent-wash-pressed`), row states (`--cursor-row`, `--cursor-bar`, `--row-hover`, `--row-zebra`, `--selected-bg`, `--on-selected`, `--marked-bg`, `--marked-bar`), the current item (`--current-bg`, `--current-text`, `--current-mark`, `--current-ring`), search matches (`--match-bg`, `--match-mark`), scrollbars, text selection, scrims, `--terminal-selection-text`, and three shadow levels (`--shadow-raised`, `--shadow-popover`, `--shadow-modal`). See `docs/themes.md`.
- Form controls (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/field`, `docs/components/field.md`): classes for native elements (`.field` with `.field-sm` and `.field-xs`, `.field-box`, `.check` with `data-tone`, `.switch`, `.range`, `.field-label`, `.field-note`, `.field-problem`, `.field-group`, `.field-choice`, `.field-grid`), a themed `meter`, and `Check` (`CheckProps`), a checkbox whose `checked` may be `'mixed'`.
- `.field-short` (`docs/components/field.md`): a text field for a short value (a port, a count, a code) is eight characters of the code face wide and starts at its column's edge, in a `.field-grid`, a dialog's form, or a line of options, instead of filling the column.
- Notices and shared recipes (`docs/components/surface.md`): `.notice` with `.notice-danger`, `.notice-warning`, `.notice-success`, and `.notice-card`; `.card`, a framed panel that groups one subject's lines (`--card-bg`); and the shared recipes in the base sheet: `.quiet-control`, `.quiet-track-item`, `.toggle-chip` (`-sm`, `-xs`), the on look of any `button[aria-pressed='true']`, `.overline`, `.scroll-fade` (`-end`, `-x`), `.hit-target`, `.selectable`, `.spinner`, and the `uiok-spin` keyframes.
- A shell stylesheet (`docs/components/shell.md`): classes for a Settings dialog (frame, sidebar, pages, subsections, a settings file's problems), a theme picker with theme miniatures, the Updates page's cards (the check cadence and the update's status, each a `.card`) and release notes, an update pill's failed state, `.channel-tag`, an About window, sign-in prompts, and a load failure block.
- `.disclosure` (`docs/components/button.md`): a disclosure chevron (the icon family's `ChevronRight`) and a plain label on a `button` with `aria-expanded`, for a section a user shows or hides, in place of a push button with a chevron.
- Icons (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/icon`, `docs/components/icon.md`): `ICON_SM`, `ICON_MD`, `ICON_LG`, and `ICON_XL` for lucide's `size` prop, and `Spinner` (`SpinnerProps`), the one turning ring.
- `DialogLayer` (`DialogLayerProps`) and `ModalDialog`'s `container` prop: dialogs rendered inside the layer mount after the content, so they paint above every pane. Dialog width steps `.dialog-size-md`, `.dialog-size-lg`, and `.dialog-size-xl`, and body classes `.dialog-body`, `.dialog-help`, `.dialog-label`, and `.dialog-value`. The `--dialog-inset-top` hook sets the backdrop's top inset. See `docs/components/dialog.md`.
- Danger tones: `Button` `variant="danger"` (`.button-danger`), `LegendSecondary.danger` (`.legend-danger`) in a dialog's key legend, and a `danger` prop on `QuietAction` and `RowIconAction`.
- `PathFact` (`PathFactProps`) in `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/pane`: a page's state at the end of a `PanePathBar`, with a tone and a mark, cut with a tooltip when the bar is narrow. See `docs/components/pane.md`.
- Tooltip options: `tooltip()` takes a `Chord` and draws it as key glyphs, and an options argument (`TooltipOptions`: `value` for a monospace value, `side` for a tooltip beside its control); `describedTooltip()` takes the same options; `cutTooltip()` opens an element's own text only while it is cut; `cutsItsText`, `tooltipChord`, `TooltipSide`, and `TooltipBeside`. `placeTooltip()` takes an optional side. See `docs/components/tooltip.md`.
- `KeyChord` `tone` (`KeyChordTone`: `accent`, `lit`, `quiet`) and `recording` props, and the `.keycap` class that draws every chord inside an element as the shared key cap. See `docs/components/keychord.md`.
- `SegmentedChoice` `disabledChoices`; its look can be drawn on plain `aria-pressed` buttons with `segmented-choice` and `segmented-choice-option`. `Pager` `size` (`'inline'`, `'small'`, `'regular'`). `DateTimePicker` `size` (`'small'` by default, `'regular'` beside a 28px field). `DataGrid` `empty`, the content of a grid with no rows, and `initialWidth`'s optional fourth argument for a sort mark's room.
- Listing row states: `.row.selected`, `.row.marked`, `.row.pane-add`, `.pane-list.pointer` for a hover wash on rows that act on a click, and `.pane-listing.pane-pin-end`, which pins the actions column while the listing scrolls sideways. A host colours its own parts of a selected row with the `--row-selected-text` and `--row-selected-text-dim` hooks, which follow the selection into its grey look. See `docs/components/listing.md`.
- Desktop menu looks (`docs/components/menu.md`): a row with `aria-checked="true"` shows a check mark in its icon column, a row with `aria-haspopup="menu"` ends in a submenu chevron, and `.folder-menu-separator` draws a plain hairline between groups.
- `.filter-field` draws a listing's filter row; `.folder-menu-compact` and the `--menu-font-size` hook size a menu; `.palette-command` sets a palette row's context as a command line; `.chart-empty`, `.chart-empty-text`, and `.chart-swatch` for charts and legends a host draws.
- `DataGrid` styles `cell-null` and `cell-binary` cells.
- `Button` `size="xs"` (`.button-xs`): the 20px push button for a row or a strip, with the 4px corner, caption words at medium weight, a 12px glyph, and a pointer target grown to `--hit-target` in the block axis. It has the strip action's values, so the library draws one 20px push button. See `docs/components/button.md`.

### Changed

- Breaking: theme tokens retired. `--shadow-menu`, `--shadow-notice`, `--shadow-card`, and `--shadow-card-hover` are gone: read `--shadow-popover`. Read `--shadow-modal` for `--shadow-dialog`, `--shadow-raised` for `--shadow-segment` and `--shadow-knob`, `--surface` (off) or `--on-primary` (on) for `--switch-knob`, and the `--tonal-hover` colour for `--tonal-hover-brightness`. `--page-tabs-fade-start` and `--page-tabs-fade-end` are gone with the tab fades. See "Retired names" in `docs/themes.md`.
- Breaking: `ThemeOverrides` takes a six-digit hex colour for colour tokens only; a factor override such as `--tonal-hover-brightness` no longer type-checks. Move a factor override into the host's own stylesheet.
- Breaking: `themeStylesheet()` throws when `extraFixed` or an app token names any token the library now defines, the new scale tokens and colour roles included (`--space-4`, `--control-border`). Rename such an app token.
- Breaking: the base stylesheet now themes elements that carry no class: a text input, textarea, or select gets the field outline and ground; a checkbox or radio gets the `.check` look; scrollbars, the caret, `accent-color`, `::selection`, `::placeholder`, and `meter` take theme colours; every focusable element gets a 2px accent ring on `:focus-visible`; a `button[aria-pressed='true']` gets the on look. These rules weigh nothing (`:where()`), so any host rule wins. To keep a native checkbox's platform drawing, give it a class.
- Breaking: the menu, palette, and match-highlight classes (`.folder-menu*`, `.entry-menu-heading`, `.palette*`, `.name-hit`) apply anywhere, not only under `.listing-root`. A host that used these class names for its own elements outside the scope now gets the library's look on them.
- Breaking: `ActivityBar` draws its own track and fill (`.activity-bar`, `.activity-fill`); `className` and `fillClassName` are optional and add to them. Remove host rules that sized or coloured the track. A determinate fill that is not `moving` now dims to `--progress-idle`: a static meter passes `moving`.
- Breaking: `DataGrid` markup. `role="grid"`, the keyboard focus, and the scrolling moved from `.data-grid` to a new `.data-grid-scroll` inside it, which holds the header row and the `rowgroup`; `.data-grid` is now the frame. Point selectors, tests, and scroll handling at `.data-grid-scroll`.
- Breaking: `PageTabs` markup. The `.page-tabs` strip sits in a `.page-tabs-frame` with `.page-tabs-more` chevron buttons at an edge that hides tabs. A tab that does not fit whole carries `data-clipped`, is not drawn, and takes no click, though it stays reachable by the arrow keys. The edge fades are gone.
- Breaking: `Pager` keeps a step that cannot act in its place as a disabled button. It used to be an empty `span.pager-step` (icon steps) or left out (worded steps). Tests that count the pager's buttons need updating.
- Breaking: `ResultChart` shows the hovered value in a `.result-chart-float` panel beside the mark and writes it to a visually hidden live line; `.result-chart-readout` is gone.
- Breaking: listing labels, row names, `StateMark` words, and `PaneStatus` summaries carry their tooltip only while the text is cut (`cutTooltip`, `data-tooltip-cut`), not always. `StateMark` wraps its word in `.pane-state-word`, draws a shape per tone (dot, diamond, square, rings), and its busy spinner is the shared `Spinner`. `QuietAction` wraps its verb in `.pane-act-word`.
- Breaking: `KeyLegendStrip` drops verbs that do not fit whole instead of clipping the last one at the edge.
- Breaking: the terminal's context menu is portalled to the document body at `--z-dialog-popover`, and `BELL_FLASH_MS` is 280 (was 250), matching `--duration-slow`.
- Breaking: `DateTimePicker`'s panel is `position: fixed` at `--z-menu` and opens above its button when the room below is short, so a scrolling pane or dialog no longer clips it.
- Every component stylesheet reads the new scale tokens and colour roles instead of fixed values, so sizes, spacing, corners, and colours line up across components. Most visual changes in this release follow from this.
- A listing lays its strip and rows on one grid (subgrid) with a name column floor (`--pane-name-floor`, 14 characters), so a narrow pane scrolls sideways instead of cutting every name. A listing with no rows hides its column labels. Rows keep their state bar and a fade at the scroller's start edge while scrolled sideways.
- Hover is one clear step from rest and a press one clear step from hover in every theme; every outlined control and key cap shares one pressed look (`--press-frost`). A disabled primary, tonal, or danger button is drawn as a disabled neutral one.
- Breaking: the pointer is the arrow over every control (buttons, tabs, rows, links, crumbs, toggles), as in a desktop application; no stylesheet sets the hand, and a dragged `DataGrid` heading no longer shows the grabbing hands. The I-beam stays in text and the resize cursors on column edges. `test/cursor.test.ts` guards it; a host that sets `cursor: pointer` on its own controls should drop it (`docs/tokens.md`, "Pointer").
- Breaking: an inline message is an application's status line, not a web alert. `.notice` drops its coloured side bar and inset: a glyph in the tone, the words in `--text`, and `.notice-actions` right after the words instead of at the far end. `.notice-card` is the window's status line under a header (the plain line, a hairline under it, no fill) instead of a framed, shadowed card. `.field-problem` and `.pane-message.danger` draw the danger glyph themselves and set their words in `--text`, not `--danger-text`. See "Where a message goes" in `docs/components/surface.md`.
- Empty text looks like what it stands in for: `Listing`'s `empty` line is `.pane-message.empty`, a quiet row starting where a row's icon starts in the rows' own type; the new `.pane-well-note` (`.beside-terminal`) sets an empty well's note in the terminal's type; `DataGrid`'s `empty` content sits where the first row would be, in the grid's cell type, instead of centred in the interface font. See "Empty" in `docs/components/listing.md`.
- Dialogs set their own type (monospace for values, the interface font for sentences and labels), cap their height to the window and scroll their body under a pinned title strip and legend, and carry the lit top edge and `--shadow-modal`.
- Breaking: a dialog's title is always one line, `ConfirmDialog`'s included: a title too long for its strip is cut with an ellipsis, and a string title carries its text as a tooltip only while cut (`cutTooltip`). A title names the action and carries no data ("Delete Now?", not "Delete 7 Items Now?"): the count, names, and paths go in the body. Nothing stands beside the title: `ModalDialog`'s `titleAside` and `ConfirmDialog`'s `context` are gone, and context goes in the body's first line. See "The title" in `docs/components/dialog.md`.
- Breaking: one confirmation anatomy for every case. `ConfirmDialog` has four parts whatever it acts on: a one-line title with no count, the lead (`message`), the things it acts on as one framed list, and the buttons. The new `items` (`DialogItem`: `name`, `detail`, `icon`), `itemIcon`, `itemCount`, and `groups` (`DialogItemGroup`: a label and count above its own list) props, and the `DialogItems` component (`DialogItemsProps`, `shownItems`, `DIALOG_ITEMS_SHOWN`), draw one row per item (the kind's glyph, the name in monospace, a dim detail at the row's end) in a framed well: five rows in full, then "+N more". A name or detail wraps, up to three lines and after a separator where it can, before it is cut. A confirmation with items is 480px wide whatever their number (`.confirm-items`), and its description reads the lead, the count, then the names. `facts` (`DialogFacts`, `DialogFact`, `DialogFactsProps`) are for facts about the action, drawn under the items; a fact's value wraps up to three lines before it is cut, and `more` ends the row in "+N more". `paths` and `DialogNames` (`DialogNamesProps`) are deprecated and draw through the same list (a path's last part as the name, its folder as the detail). The `dialog` label group gains `moreItems(count)` and `itemCount(count)`. See "One anatomy for every case" in `docs/components/dialog.md`.
- A dialog legend verb takes `help` (`DialogLegendVerb.help`), its slot's tooltip and accessible description, so a dialog says what a verb does without a sentence in its body.
- `PanePathBar` cuts every crumb in an inner `.crumb-text` and gives way in one stated order (`docs/components/pane.md`, "How a narrow bar gives way"): the crumbs before the last, the last to a stem of eight characters, a fact's words, then the crumbs before the last scroll out of view, then its quiet actions' words step down to icons (`data-tight` on the row), then the last crumb. Nothing is drawn over anything and no control leaves the row, for every fact form. A trail its row cannot hold scrolls sideways with its scrollbar hidden and fades the side that hides crumbs; a focused crumb scrolls into view clear of the fade, and `PanePathBar` rests the trail on its end, a slash first (`data-cramped`, `--pane-crumb-fit`). The bar re-fits when any box in it resizes and once fonts load. It sets `--pane-crumb-count` and `--pane-crumb-here-room` on `.pane-path-row`; a hand-written trail sets them there too.
- A path fact of bare words, or of words after a mark (a `StateMark`, a host's empty span, an icon), cuts its words in one clamped line, and `PanePathBar` gives it its words as a tooltip while cut. A host box that may cut takes `.path-fact-text`; other host boxes keep their width. A `StateMark` with `word=""` draws no gap after its mark, so a fact's mark stands one `--space-3` from its words, and a fact that opens the tools stands clear of the trail.
- Key caps are one shared look across `KeyChord`, `KeyLegendStrip`, dialog legends, and `FilterEscape`. `SegmentedChoice`, `PageTabs`, `Pager`, menus, palettes, tooltips, `RunHeader`, and `SelectAllBar` take the shared hover, press, focus, and field looks; `SelectAllBar`'s checkbox is a `.check`. `RunHeader`'s running glyph is the `Spinner` and its headline carries a tooltip.
- Syntax tokens (`--syntax-*`) and `--on-tonal` in Catppuccin Latte are adjusted to keep 4.5:1 on the ground and a panel.
- Nothing animates in: a dialog and its frosted wash appear on the next frame, with no fade, drop, or scale (the `dialog-settle` and `dialog-wash-in` keyframes are gone). A menu's `.folder-menu-note` wraps to two lines before it is cut with an ellipsis; a reason a row cannot act goes in that row's tooltip.
- `RunHeader`'s figure is 14px, the headline's size, and a failed headline keeps the text colour: only the glyph and the bar carry the danger tone. A palette's `.palette-failure` leads with the danger glyph and sets its words in `--text`.
- Breaking: a terminal that cannot start shows `.terminal-failure` as terminal text (`.terminal-failure-glyph`, `.terminal-failure-text`: the danger glyph, then the words in the terminal's monospace and text colour at its inset) instead of a `.notice.notice-danger`. Its scrollback thumb behaves as an overlay scroll bar, hidden at rest and shown while the reader scrolls (`data-scrolling`) or the pointer or keyboard is on the rail, and the pointer stays the I-beam over a link.
- Charts keep the arrow over a plot that takes a range or zoom drag (no column-resize or crosshair cursor), and the scatter frame's corner still resizes it without drawing the browser's striped grip.
- Charts share one look (axis, grid lines, swatches, hovered mark); a chart with no values keeps its plot's place and centres a dim line; numbers from a million up are written short (`2.5M`); a pie with more than eight slices hatches the later ones, and its legend ends in a `+N` count of entries it leaves out.

- Breaking: an action is never drawn as a link. `.pane-strip-link` (Update all, Clear) is a quiet strip control: dim words, the shared quiet hover and press, no accent and no underline; `.danger` stays quiet at rest and turns `--danger-text` under the pointer, with the focus, and while pressed. A lit `QuietAction` and the cursor row's actions are full words in `--text` (a lit one a weight up, its glyph in `--accent`), an add row's words turn `--text`. A `DataGrid` cell with `onOpen` leads to another record, so it keeps a link's look (`--accent-text` over an `--accent-line` underline) with the arrow pointer, and a small dim arrow at its trailing edge on the cursor cell; a download cell (`cell-binary`) keeps a quieter `--text-faint` underline in its own colour. Give `onOpen` to a value that leads out of its row, never the row's own name or id.
- Breaking: lists select as a desktop list does. Whatever marks a row (the cursor, a mark, a checked box, a selection) goes quiet in a pane that does not hold the keys and, under the new root attribute `data-window-inactive` (the host sets it while its window is not focused), in every pane, where the active pane's frame also takes `--border-strong`: the row stands on `--fill` with its words in `--text` and `--text-on-surface`, a quiet cursor has no bar, a quiet mark's bar and a checked box on the row take `--control-border-strong`. A `DataGrid` goes quiet under the same conditions: its marked rows and columns and its cursor cell take `--fill`, the cursor cell drops its ring, and its row checkboxes grey. A row's `.pane-hover-cell` icons stay in view on every row in their quiet tone instead of appearing on hover, and a busy state's ring stands still.
- An empty or failed pane says what it is and offers its actions in place: `.pane-empty` is a centred block of glyph, heading, a sentence, and its actions as buttons (`.pane-empty-actions`), now also for a pane that could not load, with the system's error under the actions in the new `.pane-empty-detail` (code face). The quiet one-line `.pane-message.empty` is for a list with nothing in it and nothing to do. Every `.pane-message` starts on the row inset, where a row's icon starts. See "Empty" in `docs/components/listing.md`.
- Breaking: row actions are glyphs; tools say their words. In a listing row the keys reach, `QuietAction` follows `RowLinkLookContext` as `RowIconAction` does: under the default `icons` it draws its glyph alone (`.pane-act.wordless`), its `word` as its tooltip (with `shortcut`) and kept for assistive technology; under `text` or `both`, which a host sets from a user's preference, its glyph and word. In a `PanePathBar`, in a `tabbable` row, and anywhere else it draws its glyph and word by default, so a command reached by Tab shows what it does; a path bar too tight for its words hides them and keeps the glyph, the word its tooltip. A listing that sized an action column for words (`ACTION_COLUMN`) can take `iconLinksColumn(1, 0)` for a glyph.
- Breaking: a `PathFact`'s `tone` colours its mark only. A failed or waiting fact's words are in `--text`, never `--danger-text` or `--warning-text`; give a toned fact its `StateMark`.
- Breaking: `PageTabs` draws its first strip as the second: a small cornered tab, the current one on `--current-bg` with a `--current-ring` edge, with no `--current-mark` underline; the strip scrolls at once, never smoothly. Each strip has room above and below its tabs: 24px tabs 6px clear of the strip's edges (a 36px strip), and in a `pills` strip 20px tabs 4px clear (28px).
- Breaking: `SelectAllBar` says how many rows are checked ("3 of 8 selected", `.select-all-count`) where it said "With selected:"; `SelectAllLabels.withSelected` is replaced by `selected(checked, total)`.
- `DataGrid` fills the width it is given, as a desktop table view fills its pane, and leaves the slack after its last column empty.
- Listings and grids select rows and cells, not text, as a file manager and a desktop table do: a drag, Shift-click, or double-click on a row never starts a text selection, even inside a region a host opts into selection, and a grid copies its cursor cell's value with Copy (`copyText`). Status summaries, pane messages, an empty grid's line, and output in a well stay selectable.
- Breaking: chrome is not selectable. The base sheet sets `user-select: none` on the body; fields, the new `.selectable` class, and each component's content (values, paths, messages and their words in `.notice-text`, `.field-problem`, a settings file's problem, a load failure, and the update card's fact, output) opt back in at zero weight; `code`, `pre`, and `samp` are chrome by their tag (a command in a label, a token in a menu), so a host marks a copyable code value `.selectable`; a listing and a grid select rows, not text. A host marks its own content `.selectable`. Selected text greys to `--fill` under `data-window-inactive` on the root.
- The arrow over every control, a link included, from a zero-weight `cursor: default` on links, buttons, labels, and control roles, so a host's own unclassed control never shows the hand.
- The Updates page keeps its cards: the check cadence and the update's status are each a `.card`, with a hairline between the status's head and its download or What's new; a failure is a headline in `--danger-text` with its glyph, the reason the system returned, and the time. The status head never shrinks under a long What's new list, so only the list gives way, and a running download's clock sits on its headline's line at the headline's size, its facts led by the percent. `.notice-card` is the window's status line under a header, with no fill.
- `.exit-confirmation-sentence` is gone: an exit confirmation is a short title, one sentence, and `ConfirmDialog`'s `facts`.
- The sign-in prompt sets its secret on the label column (`.field-grid` in `.sign-in-dialog`), not a label stacked over the field; `.field-group` is for a fieldset of choices. The About window is a fixed frame that never scrolls.
- Motion: `--duration-base` is 120ms (was 200ms) and is the longest a state change takes; `--duration-slow` is for signals only (a refusal, a bell). The update pill appears at once (its `update-pill-in` entrance is gone), a theme miniature no longer thickens its ring or drops a pixel, and a switch's knob moves at once without stretching.
- `.toggle-chip` keeps its `--control-border` outline when off, so an unpressed toggle reads as a control, and shows the filled "on" segment inside its ring when pressed.
- A number field shows a stepper at its end, a cap of its own parted from the value by a hairline with an up arrow over a down arrow (the platform's spin buttons stay the hit area), so it no longer reads as a pop-up; a textarea no longer shows a corner grip.
- Forms read as desktop forms: `.field-grid`'s label column is as wide as its longest label, capped at 40% of the form (it was a fixed 136px), and `.settings-page` keeps every Settings page on a 136px column through `--field-label-width`; a pop-up button in a `.field-value` is as wide as its longest item instead of filling the column; `.field-note` is for a line that says what the current choice does, and help that explains a control is its label's tooltip through the new `labelHelp` in `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/field` (`LabelHelp`: the tooltip on the label, the same words as the control's `aria-description`). A label with help shows a cue, a dashed underline in `--text-faint` (`data-label-help`), and its tooltip opens above it, or at its end where the window has no room above, never over the control: `TooltipSide` gains `'above'` and `placeTooltip` an eighth argument, `preferAbove`. Under `data-window-inactive` a check box or switch that is on greys to `--control-border-strong`.
- The shell's Settings: the subsection in view is bold in the text colour beside the subsections' rail, its segment of the rail in the accent; a `strong` in `.update-card-title` (a version) stays strong; the What's new list (`.update-notes-list`, `.update-notes-frame`) draws no focus ring, as a read-only text view. A window's status line (`.notice-card .notice-text`) stays on one line, cut with an ellipsis; give it `cutTooltip`.
- A tooltip wider than its control hangs from the control's leading edge (its right edge in a right-to-left page), as a desktop tooltip starts where the pointer is, so one on a short label at the start of a row no longer spreads back over the pane beside it. A glyph button keeps its tooltip centred, a control wider than its tooltip still centres it on the pointer, and the window-margin clamp and `side` are unchanged. `placeTooltip` takes an optional `rtl` after `beside`, and an optional `glyph` after it: the layer centres a tooltip only under a glyph button, told by what the control is (a button, link, tab, radio, or input no wider than it is tall), so a short label in a dialog's label column keeps its tooltip at its leading edge.
- `ConfirmDialog`'s `message` is optional: left out or `''`, no empty `.dialog-message` paragraph is drawn and the items (or else the facts) describe the dialog; with none of them, the dialog carries no `aria-describedby`. `DialogItems` and `DialogFacts` take an `id`.
- `SegmentedChoice` takes `aria-description`, the `control` half of `labelHelp(help)`, so help kept in the label's tooltip reaches a screen reader when the group takes focus, as a field's does.

### Fixed

- The terminal keeps every cell's text at 4.5:1 against that cell's background, draws selected text in `--terminal-selection-text`, shows a quiet outline cursor while unfocused, and shows a start failure in its place.
- `NameCut` never leaves a gap between an ellipsis and the next part, nor after a head shorter than an ellipsis's room (`~/` in `~/n1000/.controller 85977`), and cuts a right-to-left name the mirrored way.
- `DataGrid` keeps an open editor inside the grid's frame, and its focus ring on the frame. `DateTimePicker` keeps its time fields on one row.
- The pinned actions column and side fades follow a right-to-left `dir`; a toggle that is on stays on under the pointer; scroll fades never cover a scroller's border or scrollbar.
- The Settings folder's path (`.settings-file-path`) can be selected and copied again.
- Phase I quality restored after the desktop pass: a strip action (`.pane-strip-link`) is a small push button with a `--control-border` bezel, and its `.danger` form a `--danger` bezel, so it is never read as a column label; a cursor in a pane or window without the keys is a hairline frame in `--control-border-strong` with no fill, apart from a quiet selection's grey fill (listings and the DataGrid's cursor cell); a neutral Button, an IconButton, or a strip action that is on draws one ring; the disclosure draws the icon family's chevron and takes the shared press and the `--radius-sm` focus ring; a toned field adds a 2px bar in its tone inside its leading edge; the Updates card's progress header leads with the card's glyph size; a label column centres a label on a 28px field outside dialogs too; the column strip and a grid's header refuse text selection with the rows; the What's New list's frame draws its focus ring again; RunHeader's headline opens as a tooltip only while cut, and a failure's reason is `--text-dim`; the pane's end glyphs share one edge (`--pane-glyph-reach`); a grid editor keeps its cell's text inset; a quiet control or track item whose menu is open keeps its hovered face; a menu row may be a `button` and has a danger tone (`.folder-menu-option.danger`).
- Side effects of the restoring pass fixed: a strip action or `xs` Button in a column strip takes its height from the strip (17px in a 22px strip), so its bezel clears the strip's rules, and draws its focus ring inside, where the scroller does not clip it; only a control holding a pop-up open (`aria-expanded` with `aria-haspopup`) keeps the hovered face, so an open fold keeps its rest face; on a state row every control the shared recipes wash (a host's `.quiet-control`, `.toggle-chip`, and the rest) keeps the row's fill under its words, in listings and the DataGrid; a quiet row's busy ring and spinner take `--text-on-surface` on a quiet mark or quiet selection (the neutral fill) and keep the accent on a quiet cursor, which has no fill; the Updates card's glyph hangs on the first line of a two-line status and the status row never shrinks under a long What's New list; the DataGrid link cell's arrow is the icon family's `ArrowRight` at `--icon-sm`, not a font character.

### Removed

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-07

The first published version. The package is published to npm as `@bigpixelrocket/uiok`: install it with `npm install @bigpixelrocket/uiok` and import from `@bigpixelrocket/uiok` and `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/<area>`.

### Added

- `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/styles.css`, the base stylesheet: the `--font-ui` and `--font-mono` stacks, the `--z-panel`, `--z-dialog`, and `--z-tooltip` scale, the `--frost` backdrop filter, and the `visually-hidden` utility class.
- `VisuallyHidden`, a component for text that only assistive technology reads.
- Themes: 18 light and dark themes as generated colour tokens inside `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/styles.css`, chosen with `data-light-theme` and `data-dark-theme` on the root element (`wearThemes`, `subscribeThemeChange`). The catalog, colour helpers, token rules, and `themeStylesheet()`, whose options let an app derive and emit tokens of its own, are exported. See `docs/themes.md`.
- `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/styles.css` declares `color-scheme: light dark` and fades every disabled control to `--disabled-opacity`.
- `UiokProvider`, `useLabels`, and `mergeLabels`: shared chrome strings (`UiokLabels`), English by default, with key names and glyphs in the `keys` group.
- Core components: `Button`, `IconButton`, `TooltipLayer` with `tooltip()` and `describedTooltip()`, `ActivityBar`, `SegmentedChoice`, `KeyChord`, `FilterIcon`, and `FilterEscape`, styled through `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/styles.css`.
- Keyboard policy: `shouldSuppressBrowserDocumentKey`, `cancelBrowserDocumentKey`, `isKeyKept`, the `MODAL_SELECTOR` and `OVERLAY_SELECTOR` overlay contract, `watchOverlays`, `holdPageSelectAll`, and chord helpers (`chordSpoken`, `chordGlyphs`, `chordParts`).
- Render helpers: `createValueStore`, `unheard`, `sameData`, `sameProps`, `useStableHandlers`, `keepUnchangedById`, `keepUnchangedBy`, and `useClock`.
- Key legend: `KeyLegendStrip`, a strip of the verbs the keyboard offers under a dock of held modifiers, with app-supplied pair labels, and the `legendUnits`, `fittingUnits`, `moreLabel`, and `moreListAction` rules (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/legend`, `docs/components/legend.md`). Its chrome strings are the `legend` label group.
- Dialogs (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/dialog`): `ModalDialog`, `DialogLegend` with `legendKeyAction`, `legendSignature`, and `filterLeaveLabel`, `ConfirmDialog`, and `containTab`; the `dialog` label group (`cancel`, `clear`, `affectedItems`). Run progress (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/run`): `RunHeader`, `isSettledRun`, `runElapsedMs`, and `runClock`. See `docs/components/dialog.md` and `docs/components/run.md`.
- Listings and panes: `Listing`, `Strip`, `Row`, `Cell`, `RowCursorContext`, `StateMark`, `PathCell`, `WellColumn`, `CurrentDot`, and column widths (`column`, `ACTION_COLUMN`, `iconLinksColumn`, `linksColumn`) in `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/listing`; `PanePathBar`, `PaneStatus`, `QuietAction`, `RowIconAction`, `RowLinkLookContext`, and the two-pane keyboard `usePaneKeys` with `paneKeyAction` and its DOM helpers in `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/pane`. Styled under the `.listing-root` scope; a sorting column's accessible name comes from the new `listing` label group.
- `NameCut` with `labelParts` and `nameParts` (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/namecut`), and `MarkedText` with `highlightSegments` (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/text`).
- Data widgets: `DataGrid` with its row-window and cursor rules (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/grid`); `DateTimePicker` with `parsePicker` and `formatPicker`, ISO 8601 by default and replaceable through `format` and `parse` (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/datetime`); `Pager` with `pageCount`, `clampPage`, `pageSlice`, `pageOf`, and `selectorPages` (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/pager`); `PageTabs` (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/tabs`); `SelectAllBar` with `checkedAfterClick` (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/selectall`). Their shared words come from the new `dateTime`, `pager`, and `selectAll` label groups.
- Filtered menus and palettes: the fuzzy matcher `matchQuery` with `hasQueryTerms` and `hasNegativeTerms`, the menu rules (`menuQueryRefused`, `moveMenuCursor`, `pickableCursor`, `movedPickableCursor`, and the level trail helpers), `useBellBeat`, and the menu look in `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/menu`; `Palette` and `PaletteQuery` in `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/palette`, whose words come from the new `palette` label group.
- Charts (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/charts`): `LineChart`, `PieChart`, `ScatterChart`, and `ResultChart` (bar, column, line, spline, area, pie, timeline, scatter; stackable), painted from the `--chart-*` tokens, and `tokenColour`, `svgMarkup`, and `svgPng` to save a chart as a picture. Their shared words are the `charts` label group (`ChartLabels`).
- Each area is importable on its own as `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/<area>` (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/button`, `@bigpixelrocket/uiok/keyboard`, …).
- `lucide-react` 1.31 or later within 1.x is a peer dependency.
- Terminal (`@bigpixelrocket/uiok/terminal`, `docs/components/terminal.md`): `Terminal`, Ghostty's VT engine as WebAssembly drawn on a canvas, read-only without `onInput`, with a Copy and Paste menu whose chords come from `menuChords`, a visual bell, line-editing actions bound by `actionForKey`, and `TerminalScrollbackContext`.
